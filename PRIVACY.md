# Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

Vault Auth is a two-factor authenticator. It works entirely on your device, and it collects no data.

## What the app stores

Everything the app stores stays on your device:

- **Setup keys and account names.** They are kept in the iOS Keychain, or the Android Keystore on Android.
- **Your theme choice.** It is kept in the app's local settings.

Removing the app removes this data from the app.

## What the app does not do

- It has no user account and no sign-up.
- It has no server. Your codes are computed on your device.
- It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- It shares nothing with third parties, because it sends nothing anywhere.

## Permissions

- **Camera.** Used only to scan the QR code a service shows when you set up two-factor authentication. Images are read on the device and are not saved or sent.

## Things you choose to share

- **Copying a code** places it on the system clipboard, so you can paste it where you sign in.
- **The backup sheet** contains the setup key of every account. When you print it or save it as a PDF, your device's print and share options handle it, and it goes only where you send it. Keep the sheet private: anyone who has it can generate your codes.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
